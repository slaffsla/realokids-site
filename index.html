What it is

Small steps children can actually do.

Realo Kids is designed for children, but written for real family life: quiet encouragement, simple choices, and no pressure to perform. The goal is steady real-life action away from the screen, not endless tapping in the app.

Built-in breathing sessions use a slow, guided rhythm and original handpan music by the developer to support calmer transitions. Parents can edit missions, tune rewards to real family life, and adjust the daily schedule so the app fits the child, not the other way around.

Privacy

Offline by design.

Realo Kids stores your child’s name, progress, and settings locally on your device. The app makes no network requests and has no analytics.
