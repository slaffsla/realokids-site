What it is

Small missions. Real-life wins.

Before the screen, not instead of it. Use Realo Kids before screen time, snacks, stories, or play. One small mission, one real action, one star earned. The screen time that follows feels earned, not just consumed.

Grows with your child. Younger children get more warmth and guidance from Buddy. Older children get calmer, more independent missions and lighter celebration. One setting adjusts everything. The app follows the child. Not the other way around.

For parents

The app that holds the line, so you don't have to.

Set up Realo Kids once — missions, rewards, morning steps, daily schedule. After that, Buddy carries the daily structure. Your child hears it from the app, not from you. The boundary stays consistent. The relationship stays warm.

Privacy

Private by design. Not by policy.

Most children's apps have a privacy policy because they have to. Realo Kids has one because it says something worth saying.

No accounts. No servers. No analytics. No ads. No data collected. Your child's name, progress, missions, and routines live on your device and only on your device. The app has no backend to breach, no database to leak, no company to sell your family's data to.

It works fully offline. Always has. Always will.

Apparently, everyone loves to sell your data. We do not have any to sell.
