What it is

Small missions. Real-life wins.

Small steps children can actually do. Quiet encouragement. Simple choices. No pressure to perform.

Missions happen in real life. Stars earn real rewards you set. The app is a starting point — not the destination. Parents can edit missions, tune rewards to real family life, and adjust the daily schedule so the app fits the child, not the other way around. Breathing sessions use a slow guided rhythm and original handpan music.

Privacy

Offline by design.

Realo Kids does not collect, transmit, or store personal data. All information entered in the app is stored locally on your device and never leaves it. No analytics. No cloud tracking.

I built this first for my own child, who is neurodivergent. I wanted something simple, kind, private, and motivating — not another noisy game or complicated parent dashboard.
