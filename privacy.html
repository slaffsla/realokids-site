Privacy Policy — Realo Kids

Last updated: July 2026

Realo Kids does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

All information entered in the app — your child's name, age, progress, missions, rewards, and settings — is stored locally on your device and never leaves it.

The app makes no network requests for its core functionality. There are no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no tracking of any kind. There is no account to create and no server to send data to.

This app is designed for use by children under parental supervision. No information is collected from child users.

Nothing leaves your phone. There is nothing to breach because there is nothing stored anywhere but your own device.
